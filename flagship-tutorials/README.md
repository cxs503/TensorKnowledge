# Flagship Tutorial Portfolio

These are initial tutorial briefs, not completed or validated courses. Each must be developed using the tutorial specification and pass content and learning review before being labeled complete.

| # | Working title | Main domains | Intended capstone |
|---|---|---|---|

| 3 | Data, Evidence, and Uncertainty | 2, 1 | Audit a dataset and communicate uncertainty |
| 4 | Models, Systems, and Emergence | 3, 4 | Analyze a system with feedback and limits |
| 5 | Interdisciplinary Thinking and Knowledge Transfer | 4 | Solve a problem using ideas from two domains |
| 6 | Working with AI Agents Without Surrendering Judgment | 1, 5, 6 | Complete and audit an agent-assisted task |

| 10 | Ethics, Institutions, and Civilizational Futures | 6, 4 | Compare futures and justify a design choice |

## Selection criteria

Prioritize tutorials that teach transferable foundations; can be practiced with accessible tools and data; demonstrate value and limits of agent assistance; connect multiple representations; have assessable outcomes; and can be reviewed by qualified people.

## Status convention

Use proposed, draft, in-review, piloted, validated, or deprecated. A tutorial is not validated merely because it is published or an agent reports completion.
