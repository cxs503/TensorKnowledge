## 1. Driving question and purpose

What meaningful question will the learner investigate, explain, or act on? Why does it matter?

## 2. Observable learning outcomes

By the end, the learner can:
- [ ] explain ...
- [ ] interpret or derive ...
- [ ] evaluate ...
- [ ] transfer ...
- [ ] create or investigate ...

## 3. Concept and pathway map

List key concepts, prerequisites, related tutorials, and links across knowledge domains. Explain dependencies; do not imply that every learner must follow one fixed sequence.

## 4. Foundations

Define necessary vocabulary and prior ideas. Distinguish essential prerequisites from optional depth.

## 5. Core explanation

Explain mechanisms and reasoning. Separate established claims, interpretations, hypotheses, and contested issues. State assumptions and scope.

## 6. Multiple representations

Use only representations that add learning value: text, equations, diagrams, data, code, simulations, images, audio/video, or physical demonstrations. Record provenance, units, and transformations.

## 7. Worked example or case study

Show the reasoning process, not only the final answer. Include at least one limitation or alternative interpretation.

## 8. Practice and feedback

Provide tasks that require active learner work. Include expected reasoning, common errors, and useful feedback.

## 9. Agent collaboration

Specify what the agent may do, what the learner must do, what needs independent verification, and one plausible agent failure challenge.

## 10. Transfer and creation

Ask the learner to apply the ideas to an unfamiliar context, formulate a new question, or produce and validate an artifact.

## 11. Assessment

Map each outcome to evidence and a rubric. Do not assess only polished output or agent-generated artifacts.

## 12. Sources, data, and reproducibility

List verifiable references, datasets, licenses, collection conditions, software, parameters, and instructions needed to reproduce central examples.

## 13. Limitations, safety, and accessibility

Record uncertainty, excluded cases, safety considerations, privacy issues, alternative access routes, and known barriers.
